Ongoing Manuscript in preparation Imperial College London · Oct 2025–present

Prescription‑Mediated Multi‑Morbidity Index (PMMI)

Established comorbidity indices, such as Charlson and Elixhauser, are built from recorded diagnoses. PMMI is built instead from primary-care prescription records in UK Biobank. We ask whether prescriptions add information on all-cause mortality risk beyond these diagnosis-based indices.

  1. DataPrescriptions grouped by British National Formulary (BNF) category; analyses stratified by sex, each with its own category set
  2. Variable selectionElastic-net penalised Cox regression, alone and within stability selection (naive and calibrated with SHARP)
  3. BenchmarkRandom survival forests, a non-parametric comparator that allows for non-linear effects and interactions
  4. ScoreAn integer risk score built from the selected categories

Supervisors: Prof. Marc Chadeau-Hyam and Dr Dragana Vuckovic. With Dr Rin Wada. Developed from my bachelor’s thesis.

Completed School of Biomedical Engineering and Imaging Sciences, King’s College London · Jul–Aug 2025

Automated spatial‑resolution measurement for fluoroscopy quality assurance

Routine quality assurance of fluoroscopy systems includes imaging a resolution test phantom and reading the system’s limiting spatial resolution by eye, which is slow and varies between observers.

I wrote a MATLAB pipeline that automates this reading for the Leeds TOR 18FG phantom, at about one second per image. Its estimates of limiting resolution were about 50% higher in line pairs per millimetre (lp/mm) than observers’ visual readings: it placed the limit at finer bar groups than observers reported by eye. They were checked against measurements made in ImageJ.

  1. InputDICOM images, preprocessed before analysis
  2. LocalisationPhantom rim located by Canny edge detection and a Hough transform
  3. AlignmentRotation corrected by Powell optimisation and a Radon transform
  4. MeasurementContrast in each of the 21 bar-pattern groups; limiting resolution in lp/mm taken at a 10% contrast threshold

Supervisor: Prof. Kawal Rhode. Thanks to Carlo Saija for MATLAB advice.
